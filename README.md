## About Computer Vision 🌟

Computer Vision is an exciting field that teaches machines to “see” and interpret the world just like humans do! By analyzing images and videos, computers can recognize objects, understand scenes, and even make decisions. This subject opens up endless possibilities, from self-driving cars 🚗 to smart medical diagnostics 🏥. Dive into this lab to unleash the power of visual intelligence and transform pixels into meaningful insights!

## Experiments List 🔍

1. **Implementing Various Basic Image Processing Operations** 🖼️  
Master the essentials of image reading, writing, and format conversions with hands-on exercises.

2. **Contrast Adjustment of an Image** 🎨  
Bring dull images to life by enhancing their contrast and making details pop!

3. **Histogram Processing and Equalization** 📊  
Learn how to balance image brightness and contrast for clearer visual outputs.

4. **Low Pass and High Pass Filtering Mechanisms** ⚙️  
Explore smoothing and sharpening filters to polish images for better analysis.

5. **Use of Fourier Transform for Filtering Images** 🔄  
Delve into the frequency domain to filter images in powerful new ways.

6. **Utilization of SIFT and HOG Features for Image Analysis** 🔍  
Extract standout features that help computers recognize and classify images effectively.

7. **Image Segmentation Algorithms** ✂️  
Divide images into meaningful parts to simplify complex visual information.

8. **Optical Flow Computation Algorithm** ⏱️  
Track movement across frames and bring videos to life with motion analysis.

9. **Face Detection on Available Online Human Face Image Datasets** 👤  
Detect and locate faces, a foundation for many biometric and security applications.

10. **Object Recognition on Available Online Image Datasets** 🎯  
Train models to identify and classify objects accurately in a variety of images.
